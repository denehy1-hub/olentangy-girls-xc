❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

+

Transportation to Off-Site Practices

If she doesn't drive yet, she'll be assigned to another team member who is a Senior or Junior with a driver's license to safely transport her. Off-site practices happen at Highbanks Metro Park, Alum Creek Below the Dam, Marina/Beach, or Liberty Park (Wednesday/Friday practices and lifting are at OHS).

Race Day Expectations

Athletes run a 5k (3.1 miles). Athletes must ride the team bus to meets and sign out with parents before leaving. Parents should NOT enter the athlete tent area during the 45 minutes before a meet and 30 minutes after.

Where should parents hang out during races?

Visit the Parent Tent, set up away from the team area, where parents mingle, check viewing locations, enjoy snacks, and celebrate accomplishments.
