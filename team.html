Program Philosophy: "Above the Line" & "Ohana"

Our program operates on the principle of being "Above the Line"—making intentional, positive choices that elevate ourselves and those around us.

We also embrace the concept of "Ohana", meaning family. No runner stands alone; we train together and support one another on and off the course.
