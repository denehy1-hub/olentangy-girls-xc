Coaches & Team Roster

Meet the dedicated coaching staff and the exceptional student-athletes representing Olentangy Girls Cross Country for the 2026 season.

Coaching Staff

Experienced leadership fostering athletic endurance, teamwork, and character development.

Coach Dewese

Head Coach

Dedicated to building competitive distance runners while emphasizing sportsmanship, academic excellence, and lifelong fitness habits.

Read Full Biography +

Dewese brings an incredible 45 years of coaching experience to the table, with 41 years spent at Olentangy! He has passionately coached cross country, track, volleyball, and basketball, inspiring countless athletes along the way.

A proud graduate of The Ohio State University with a degree in education, Coach Dewese comes from a family of educators—both of his parents were teachers, and his father also coached basketball and baseball! As one of four boys with names all starting with "D," he has a fun and unique family dynamic.

Coach and his wonderful wife, Caroline, have celebrated over 43 years of marriage and are proud parents of a daughter who teaches at Oak Creek, along with a grandson who is currently thriving in high school within the district!

Coach Sosa

Assistant Coach

Focuses on technical training pacing strategies, injury prevention, and building a supportive team culture across all grade levels.

Read Full Biography +

Coach Sosa is an inspiring educator at Olentangy High School, bringing creativity and enthusiasm to her students every day! A proud graduate of The Ohio State University in Art Education, she encourages young artists through interactive classes such as Art 1, Sculpture, Modeling and Animation, Digital Art and Design, and AP Art History.

As she enters her exciting fourteenth year at OHS, she has also devoted 13 wonderful years to coaching the girls track team and is preparing for another phenomenal season with the cross country team.

Originally from New York, she and her husband John are the proud parents of three energetic boys, adding even more joy to their lively family life!
