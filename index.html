Email: jennifer_sosa@olsd.us

Coach Sosa focuses on conditioning, technique refinement, and motivating athletes to achieve their personal bests throughout the cross country season.

Program Philosophy: "Above the Line" & "Ohana"

Our program operates on the principle of being "Above the Line"—making intentional, positive choices that elevate ourselves and those around us.

We also embrace the concept of "Ohana", meaning family. No runner stands alone; we train together and support one another on and off the course.

Parent & Booster Resource Center

2026 Season Info

Click below to expand any section for camp details, fee breakdowns, traditions, and frequently asked questions.

Dates: Thursday, July 30th and Friday, July 31st, 2026

Location: Camp Lazarus (4422 Columbus Pike, Delaware, OH 43015)

Time: Begins each morning at 8:00 AM

Details: Team members should pack a lunch. Dinner will be provided. A Medical Release Form is required for every participant.

When: Following Camp on Friday, July 31st, 2026 at 5:30 PM

Events: Family dinner followed by a short meeting presented by the boosters and team camp skits. Please plan to attend!

Questions? Email boosters at olentangygirlsxc@gmail.com

Booster & Camp Fee: $120 fee due by July 29, 2026. Covers camp costs, food, facility use, t-shirts, senior banners, replacement uniforms, website, and End-of-Year Celebration.

Payment Methods:

  • Check: Written to OAB with “OHS Girls XC Camp and Booster Fee” in memo line.
  • Cash: Give to Coaching Staff in a sealed envelope with athlete's name.
  • Zelle ID: ohsgxctreasurer@gmail.com (designate runner's name).
  • Venmo: @ohsgirlsxc (designate runner's name).

Required Forms: OHSAA Physical Form and FinalForms registration must be completed prior to mandatory practice.

Pasta Dinners: Every Thursday after practice. Hosted at homes or parks. Team arrives around 5:00 PM. Boosters provide serving ware and pasta (scheduled with Carrabba’s). Hosts provide drinks and desserts.

Chocolate Milk Tradition: One gallon provided by boosters after every meet to replenish muscle tissue with an optimal 3:1 carb-to-protein recovery ratio.

Transportation to Off-Site Practices

If she doesn't drive yet, she'll be assigned to another team member who is a Senior or Junior with a driver's license to safely transport her. Off-site practices happen at Highbanks Metro Park, Alum Creek Below the Dam, Marina/Beach, or Liberty Park (Wednesday/Friday practices and lifting are at OHS).

Race Day Expectations

Athletes run a 5k (3.1 miles). Athletes must ride the team bus to meets and sign out with parents before leaving. Parents should NOT enter the athlete tent area during the 45 minutes before a meet and 30 minutes after.

Where should parents hang out during races?

Visit the Parent Tent, set up away from the team area, where parents mingle, check viewing locations, enjoy snacks, and celebrate accomplishments.
